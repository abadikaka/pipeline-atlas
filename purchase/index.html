Pipeline Atlas is being prepared for the Mac App Store. No payment is collected on this website.

Price and access

The planned US storefront price is US$19.99 upfront. There is no subscription, in-app purchase, license key, or device activation. Apple may display a different local currency and applicable taxes. Your App Store purchase gives you access to this listing and its future updates.

How it will work

  1. Open the verified Pipeline Atlas listing in the Mac App Store.
  2. Buy and install through your Apple Account.
  3. Open Pipeline Atlas and explore the example or choose a repository. The full tool is available immediately.

TestFlight will provide free access to invited beta testers. The older direct beta uses a different trial and license system and is not the App Store edition. Export a JSON report there, then import it through Report History in the App Store app.
