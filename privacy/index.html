This website

The interactive example uses fictional configuration in your browser. It does not connect to, analyze, or upload your repositories. This site adds no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or mailing-list forms. Your appearance preference is stored in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages at michaelabadi.com. GitHub processes connection information needed to serve it and may retain operational logs under its own policies. GitHub privacy statement.

The Mac app

Pipeline analysis runs on your Mac. Authorized GitLab and GitHub connections retrieve configuration from your provider. Saved credentials are stored in macOS Keychain; settings and reports remain local unless you export or share them. Company proxies and gateways process traffic according to your IT configuration.

A report can contain confidential job names, paths, commands and configuration. Review it before sharing. Do not save secrets in scenario variables. The app never executes repository scripts to resolve generated configuration.
